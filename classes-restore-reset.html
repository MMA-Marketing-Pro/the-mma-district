Program 06 · Restore & Reset

Restore & Reset. Undo The Week.

A dedicated recovery class: release deep tension, restore joint mobility, and reset body and mind.

Restorative Yoga Breathwork Myofascial Release
Why Restore & Reset

Hard training and desk-bound days leave the same marks: locked hips, stiff shoulders, a nervous system stuck in the on position. Restore & Reset is the session that reverses it — restorative yoga, deep breathwork, and targeted myofascial release, combined to leave you loose, balanced, and fully recharged.

What The Class Covers
  1. Restorative yoga: long, supported holds that let tissue actually let go
  2. Deep breathwork: down-regulating the nervous system out of fight-or-flight
  3. Myofascial release: foam roller and ball work into the fascia that training binds up
  4. Joint mobility: restoring range at the hips, shoulders, spine, and ankles
  5. Reset: leaving with a body that moves freely and a head that's quiet

Class Flow

Each session: breath and settling → guided myofascial release → supported restorative holds → mobility work → a final reset. Low intensity throughout. You leave calmer than you arrived, not more tired.

Who It's For

Fighters recovering between hard sessions, and anyone carrying the strain of daily stress. No training background and no flexibility required — this is the class you take precisely because you're tight.

After Hard Training

The best pairing is a hard week. Sliding one Restore & Reset session in alongside your regular classes is what keeps the nagging stuff from turning into time off the mats.

What to Bring

Comfortable clothes you can lie down in, water, and a towel. Mats, blocks, straps, rollers, and balls are all provided. Trained barefoot or in socks — your preference.

Scaling & Injuries

Every position has a supported version, and pressure work is always yours to control. Tell the coach what's hurt before class and the session gets built around it.
